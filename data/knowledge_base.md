# Hirevo: knowledge base (test content)

Fictional product facts for the reply agent. Nothing here describes a real company. The agent may only answer from these sections and must escalate anything else.

## time_to_shortlist
A first shortlist of three to five vetted candidates is usually shared within 10 business days of the role brief. Timing for senior or niche roles is confirmed on the call.

## roles_covered
Hirevo recruits for permanent roles in tech, sales, operations and HR, from mid-level to head-of level. It does not place temporary or hourly staff.

## process
Each role starts with a 30-minute brief with the hiring manager. One recruiter owns the role, screens candidates for skills and fit, and sends a progress update every week.

## replacement_policy
If a placed candidate leaves within the first 90 days, Hirevo restarts the search for that role at no extra fee. Details are in the contract.

## data_and_privacy
Candidate data is stored on servers in the EU and handled under GDPR. A data processing agreement is available on request before signing.

## minimum_size
Hirevo works with companies of 20 employees or more.

## support
Recruiters are available by email and phone on weekdays from 09:00 to 18:00 CET. Each client gets one named recruiter per role.

## not_covered_on_purpose
Fees are shared on a call and there is no public price list. Hirevo never promises a hire or a fixed time-to-hire in writing. Questions about specific past clients go to a sales rep.
